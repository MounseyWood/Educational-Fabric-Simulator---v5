Interactive Fabric Background

This website features a dynamic fabric simulation that responds to your interactions. The fabric hangs from the four corners of your screen, affected by gravity and wind forces.

Scroll down to create wind effects that buffet the fabric. Move your cursor to influence the direction of the wind across the screen.

How It Works

The fabric is simulated using a physics engine with Verlet integration, calculating realistic cloth movement through constraint-based dynamics.

As you scroll, the velocity and direction of your scrolling generates wind forces that push and pull on the fabric, creating natural, flowing motion.

Your cursor position influences the directional wind - move it left or right, up or down, and watch the fabric respond to your movements.

Geometric Textile

The overlaid texture visualization shows both the smooth surface of the fabric and its underlying structural geometry, creating a beautiful interplay between form and function.

The fabric's behavior is influenced by properties like stretch, shear, bending, and weight - all working together to create realistic cloth simulation.

Keep Scrolling

Notice how the fabric continues to move and settle even after you stop scrolling. The physics simulation includes damping and natural oscillation, making the motion feel organic and lifelike.

Try scrolling quickly, then slowly. Move your mouse around while scrolling. Each interaction creates a unique pattern of movement in the fabric.

End of Demo

This demonstrates how the fabric simulation can be used as an engaging background for any website content.

The combination of scroll-based and cursor-based interactions creates a rich, immersive experience that responds naturally to user input.
